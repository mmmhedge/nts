# Privacy Policy — Tuna

Tuna is an unofficial browser extension for nts.live.

**Data collected:** None.

**Data stored:** Your favorite stations and recently played stations are saved locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your device.

**Network requests:** Tuna makes requests only to `nts.live` and `ntslive.net` to fetch stream URLs and show metadata. No requests are made to any third-party analytics or tracking services.

**Data shared:** Nothing is shared with anyone.
